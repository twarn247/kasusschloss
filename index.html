The sorting shortcut

For speed you only need one question per form. Ends in m or r (dem, der, einem, einer): Dativ. Ends in e or s, or bare ein (die, das, eine, ein): Akkusativ. Ends in n: look at the noun. Singular is Akkusativ (den Hund, einen Hund), plural is Dativ (den Kindern).
